# Retail Sales & Business Performance Analytics

## Business Problem

A retail business needs a comprehensive understanding of its sales performance, profitability, product performance, regional performance, and customer purchasing patterns.

The objective is to analyze historical retail transactions and convert the data into actionable insights that support better business decisions.

## Project Objectives

1. Analyze overall sales and profitability.
2. Identify high-performing and underperforming products.
3. Evaluate category and subcategory performance.
4. Analyze regional and geographical sales.
5. Study sales trends over time.
6. Analyze customer segments and purchasing patterns.
7. Evaluate the relationship between discounts and profit.
8. Identify business opportunities and risks.
9. Develop an interactive Power BI dashboard.
10. Provide data-driven recommendations to management.

## Key Business Questions

### Sales Performance
- What is total sales revenue?
- How many orders were placed?
- How many units were sold?
- What is the average order value?
- How are sales changing monthly and annually?

### Profitability
- What is total profit?
- What is the overall profit margin?
- Which products generate the highest profit?
- Which products generate high sales but low profit?
- Which categories or regions operate at a loss?

### Product Performance
- Which categories generate the most revenue?
- Which subcategories generate the highest profit?
- Which products are best sellers?
- Which products perform poorly?

### Regional Performance
- Which regions generate the most revenue?
- Which states perform best?
- Which geographical areas have low profitability?

### Customer Analysis
- Which customer segments contribute the most revenue?
- Which customers generate the highest profit?
- What is the average order value across segments?

### Discount Analysis
- How do discounts affect sales?
- How do discounts affect profit?
- Are higher discounts associated with lower profitability?
- Which categories are most affected by discounts?

### Time-Based Analysis
- What are monthly sales trends?
- What are seasonal patterns?
- Which months perform best?
- How does performance change year over year?
